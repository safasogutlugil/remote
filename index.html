Connect to your TV

  1. Turn the TV on and keep this phone on the same Wi-Fi.

  3. Allow the TV’s security certificate. Your browser will say the connection isn’t private: tap Advanced (Show details on iPhone), then Proceed (visit this website). A blank or error page after that is normal. Come back to this tab.

  4. Connect, then accept the prompt on the TV with its own remote. If your browser asks to access devices on your local network, allow it.

Browsers can’t send the signal that turns a TV on, so use the TV’s own remote for that. This remote takes over once it’s on.
